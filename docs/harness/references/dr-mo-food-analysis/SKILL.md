## ⚗️ PHASE 2: CHEMICAL TYPESETTING, EQUATIONS & STRUCTURE DRAWING

Inspired by the GitHub `ghutchis/chem-skill` cheminformatics architecture and official IUPAC/ACS publishing guidelines, all chemical entities must follow strict formatting rules:

### 2.1 The Cardinal Rule: Upright (Roman) vs. Math Italic
In chemistry, letters represent chemical elements and compounds—**never** mathematical variables. Mathematical variables are italicized ($x, y, m, T$), whereas chemical element symbols are **strictly upright / Roman**:

| Formulation | Status | Correct Syntax | Rendered Output |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `($CO_2$)` | ❌ **FORBIDDEN** (Italic math variables: $C \times O_2$) | `CO<sub>2</sub>` or `$\mathrm{CO}_2$` or `\ce{CO2}` | $\mathrm{CO}_2$ |
| `($(NH_4)_2C_2O_4$)` | ❌ **FORBIDDEN** (Italic elements & brackets) | `(NH<sub>4</sub>)<sub>2</sub>C<sub>2</sub>O<sub>4</sub>` or `$\mathrm{(NH_4)_2C_2O_4}$` or `\ce{(NH4)2C2O4}` | $\mathrm{(NH_4)_2C_2O_4}$ |
| `$Ca^{2+}$` | ❌ **FORBIDDEN** (Italic calcium) | `Ca<sup>2+</sup>` or `$\mathrm{Ca}^{2+}$` or `\ce{Ca^{2+}}` | $\mathrm{Ca}^{2+}$ |
| `$C_2O_4^{2-}$` | ❌ **FORBIDDEN** (Italic oxalate) | `C<sub>2</sub>O<sub>4</sub><sup>2−</sup>` or `$\mathrm{C}_2\mathrm{O}_4^{2-}$` or `\ce{C2O4^{2-}}` | $\mathrm{C}_2\mathrm{O}_4^{2-}$ |

### 2.2 Chemical Reaction Equations & Decomposition Sequences
All reaction equations must clearly feature upright symbols, stoichiometric coefficients, ionic charges, reaction arrows with conditions, precipitation arrows ($\downarrow$), and gas evolution arrows ($\uparrow$).

- ✅ **Standard Publication KaTeX / LaTeX Syntax (Upright Roman)**:
  ```latex
  $$\mathrm{Ca}^{2+} + \mathrm{C}_2\mathrm{O}_4^{2-} \longrightarrow \mathrm{CaC}_2\mathrm{O}_4\downarrow$$
  $$\mathrm{CaC}_2\mathrm{O}_4 \xrightarrow{550^\circ\mathrm{C}} \mathrm{CaCO}_3 + \mathrm{CO}\uparrow \xrightarrow{1000^\circ\mathrm{C}} \mathrm{CaO} + \mathrm{CO}_2\uparrow$$
  ```
- ✅ **Standard `mhchem` Syntax (`\ce{...}`)**:
  ```latex
  $$\ce{Ca^{2+} + C2O4^{2-} -> CaC2O4 v}$$
  $$\ce{CaC2O4 ->[550^\circ\text{C}] CaCO3 + CO ^ ->[1000^\circ\text{C}] CaO + CO2 ^}$$
  ```

### 2.3 2D Molecular Structure Generation via SMILES (`chem_draw.py`)
To depict structural formulas for fatty acids, amino acids, carbohydrates, vitamins, and adulterants (e.g., melamine, Sudan dye), use the integrated `chem_draw.py` script:
