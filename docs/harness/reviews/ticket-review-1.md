# Codex review of TICKET.md (2026-09-25, read-only)

> Verbatim final report, codex-cli 0.153.3. Triage pending.

## Overall verdict: **Not ready**

The ticket captures much of the intended architecture, but it is not yet safe for context-free execution. Three blockers remain:

- STEP 6 declares universal gate enforcement before the direct legacy entry points are secured.
- The second, hash-bound approval after the design stage is missing.
- Arabic and translation architecture is deferred until after the core schemas, state machine, renderer, and figure interfaces are implemented.

The earlier review is partly reflected: explicit ingest, brief/design separation, pre-evaluation rubric approval, intake hashes, structured goldens, mutation tests, and Arabic locale requirements all appear. However, ingest fidelity, downstream hash binding, post-design approval, and artifact-level regression evidence are incomplete or weakened.
