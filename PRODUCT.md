# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: one self-contained static HTML file (the user asked for exactly one page; no framework needed).

## Users

- University faculty members who own a course book, memo or lab manual and want a respectable, standards-grade edition.
- Departments and faculties that may contract whole course sets (secondary path).
Audience is Arabic-speaking (Egypt); page copy is Arabic, RTL.

## Product Purpose

A book-production service built on the book harness: take any university textbook, in any science, in Arabic or English, and run it through six gated phases: ingest, evaluate (find gaps and problems), design a stronger and simpler edition, rewrite (translation Arabic⇄English first when needed), build Word and PDF, re-score. Success: the teacher receives a cleaned, corrected, better-taught edition that meets the academic standards of its own discipline.

## Positioning

Not a copy shop with a photocopier and a binding machine. Every book is scored against a discipline-specific rubric before and after, every finding is logged and fixed, and nothing ships until the author has approved the brief and the design. The edge is the measured, gated editorial process, not the printing.

## Operating Context

Deliverables: DOCX + PDF. Supports chemical structures, reaction schemes, charts and diagrams. Author approval gates at intake and design.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Any science; Arabic and English; translation between them.
- Score before/after against a rubric; findings ledger; errata ledger.
- No pricing shown. Primary CTA: WhatsApp (number is a placeholder to be filled).

## Brand Commitments

Brand name not decided — use a clearly temporary placeholder name.

## Evidence on Hand

The user declined showing the real CNS pharmacology case study, its numbers, pages or university logos. All examples on the page must be illustrative and labeled as examples. No testimonials, client names, counts of books delivered, or prices may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Measured, not promised: show the before/after score mechanism.
2. The author stays in charge: nothing moves without their approval.
3. Discipline-specific standards, not one generic polish.
4. Arabic and English as equals.
