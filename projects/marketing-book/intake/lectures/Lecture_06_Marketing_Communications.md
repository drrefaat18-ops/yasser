## Purposes of Marketing Communications in Pharmaceutical Companies

1. Attract the attention of specialists.
2. Provide information about the properties and benefits of the medication.
3. Achieve prescription of Rx medication by doctors, or OTC recommendations by pharmacists.
4. Neutralize competing companies' advertising.
5. Create a brand image for the medication and experts' trust in it.

## Means of Communication in Pharmaceutical Companies

1. Personal communication channels.
2. Non-personal communication channels — print, radio, television, creating a certain environment, events (press conferences, etc.).
3. Opinion leaders (WHO, FDA, …).
4. Recently, social media platforms — websites, Facebook, Twitter, YouTube — and the firms' digital engagement strategies.

## Factors Affecting the Use of Marketing Communications

1. **Goals and objectives** — different goals require different means of promotion.
2. **Type of product** — affects the choice of means, target audience, cost, size and nature of the market segment.
3. **Nature of the promotion strategy** — push strategy or pull strategy.

## Promotion

**Promotion** is creating and supporting long-term relations between the enterprise and the market, to activate selling of goods and create a positive image through information, persuasion and reminders about its activities.

### Benefits of Promotion Policy

- Increase sales
- Enhance marketing efficiency
- Increase business profitability

### Purposes of Promotion

- Inform prospective customers about goods, services, etc.
- Convince the buyer to prefer these products.
- Make the buyer act now on what the market offers, rather than delay the purchase.
- Build loyalty to the brand.
- Stimulate the process of buying goods, and others.

### Promotion Strategies

| Push strategy | Pull strategy |
|---|---|
| Manufacturer → Wholesaler → Retailer → Consumer | Consumer → Retailer → Wholesaler → Manufacturer |
| The product is pushed down the channel by each member to the next | Consumer demand pulls the product up through the channel (the manufacturer communicates directly with consumers) |

## Sales Promotion of Pharmaceutical Goods

Unlike other marketing-communication tools, sales promotion can **quickly affect supply and demand** and immediately change consumer behaviour.

**Sales-promotion tools for buyers in traditional marketing:**

1. Financial
2. Credit
3. Distribution or delivery of free samples (sampling)
4. Premium sales, coupons
5. Competitions and lotteries among buyers
6. Use of packaging
7. Expanded sales campaigns
8. Service policy

## Advertising

**Advertising** is a paid, non-personal communication carried out by an identified sponsor through the media to persuade or influence an audience.

**Advertising can be done in three ways:**

1. In-house advertising
2. Advertising to build the company's prestige in the community (public relations)
3. Digital advertising

### Classification of Advertisements

| By geographical spread | By target audience | By media | By function |
|---|---|---|---|
| National | Consumers | Print media | To inform |
| International | Industrial | Electronic media | To remind |
| Local | Professional | Online media | To persuade |
| Regional | | Outdoor media | |

### Components of the Advertising Process

1. **Advertisers (clients):** a person or company that pays for a product, event or job to be advertised online, in a newspaper, on television or on a poster.
2. **Advertising agencies:** responsible for initiating, managing and implementing paid marketing communications.
3. **Means of advertising (advertising package):**
   - Traditional: newspapers, magazines, TV and radio programmes, direct mail, posters and miscellaneous media.
   - In the 21st century, advertisers increasingly use digital technology (websites, Facebook, YouTube) to draw attention to products.
4. **Consumers (advertising audience):** the target audience — the specific group of consumers most likely to want the product or service; may be defined by age, gender, income, location, interests or other factors.

### Functions of Advertising Medicines

1. **Provide the most current advertising market** — find the most up-to-date statistics and facts about the global advertising market.
2. **Expand the scope of a particular drug** — new indications or new formulations.
3. **Expand the market** — sell a product in a new market to serve a different customer need.

## Advertising vs. Sales Promotion (Traditional Marketing)

| | Advertising | Sales Promotion |
|---|---|---|
| **Time** | Long term | Short term |
| **Definition** | One-way communication of a persuasive message by an identified sponsor, whose purpose is non-personal promotion of products/services to potential customers | Usually involves an immediate incentive for a buyer (intermediate distributor or end consumer); can also involve spreading information about a product, product line, brand or company |
| **Price** | Expensive | Not very expensive |
| **Suitable for** | Medium to large companies | Small to large companies |
| **Sales** | Assumption that it will lead to sales | Directly related to sales |
| **Example** | A newspaper advertisement about a company's major products | Free products, coupons, etc. |
| **Purpose** | Increase sales, brand building | Increase sales |
| **Result** | Slowly | Very soon |
