## The Marketing Concept

Marketing is **the process of planning and executing the conception, pricing, promotion, and distribution of goods, services, and ideas to create exchanges that satisfy individual and organizational objectives.**

### Digital Marketing

Also known as **online marketing**: advertising delivered through digital channels to promote brands and connect with potential customers using the internet and other forms of digital communication, such as:

- Search engines
- Websites
- Social media
- Email
- Mobile apps

## Components of the Marketing Process

1. Needs, wants, demands
2. Products and services
3. Value, satisfaction and quality
4. Exchange, transactions and relationships

## The Scope of Marketing

### 1. Create Awareness

- Informing customers about the company's products is a must for attracting them to buy.
- Marketing is the medium through which companies communicate with the public and explain the features or benefits of their products.

### 2. Study What Customers Want

- Marketing helps in understanding the needs or wants of customers, which enables companies to provide satisfactory services.
- Through their marketing programs, businesses interact with customers and understand their behaviour.
- A proper understanding of customer demand helps in designing the right product to satisfy their needs.

### 3. Product Planning

- A product is **a bundle of benefits that offers satisfaction to consumers**.
- Product planning starts with the generation of the idea and continues until the product is ready to be launched.
- To create a successful product, the company must understand consumer needs and the competition currently available in the market.

### 4. After-Sales Services

- The processes that make sure customers are satisfied with the organization's products and services.
- They ensure products and services meet or exceed customer expectations.
- **Examples of after-sales service:**
  - Warranty information
  - Product or service training
  - Repair or upgrade offers
  - Request for review
  - Return and exchange policy details
  - Automated support resources
  - Coupon or digital code
  - Survey

### 5. Pricing Policies

- **Price** is the amount of money charged for a product or service, or the sum of the values consumers exchange for the benefits of having or using it.
- Pricing involves the marketer determining the optimum price for a product.
- Good pricing policies help attract more consumers.
- Consumers are generally **price elastic**: the lower the price, the higher the demand; the higher the price, the lower the demand.
- Factors that influence price: manufacturing cost, government policies, marketing, competitors' prices, etc.

### 6. Distribution

- Selecting the proper distribution channel is necessary to attract new consumers.
- Choosing a channel means defining the route the goods take from the producer to the ultimate consumer.
- **Wholesaling** and **retailing** are the two most popular distribution channels.

### 7. Selling

- Selling is the supply of goods and services, directly or indirectly, to target consumers.
- It involves managing several activities at once: approaching new consumers, distributing free samples, making sales at big discounts, and getting feedback.

### 8. Packaging

- Packaging delivers the product safely and builds a good image in the consumer's mind.
- It involves designing and producing the external covering that keeps the product safe and hygienic.
- Packaging carries product information, which adds to the product's appeal and helps sales promotion.

### 9. Advertising

- Advertising is the best tool for marketing; it makes consumers aware of the product about to hit the market.
- It can increase sales drastically, and ultimately profits.
- Media: newspapers, television, radio, magazines, hoardings (billboards), the internet, etc.

### 10. Collecting Feedback

- Collecting customers' feedback or suggestions once the product is sold.
- This identifies customer satisfaction or dissatisfaction and helps improve service quality using their suggestions.

## Marketing Mix (4 Ps)

**Marketing mix:** "the set of actions, or tactics, that a company uses to promote its brand or product in the market."

**Marketing mix principles** — also known as the 4 Ps: "the key factors involved in the marketing of a good or service": **Product, Price, Place, Promotion.**

### 1. Product

- "Product" refers to the functions and features of a good or service.
- It should satisfy the needs of the customer.
- It may have a unique selling proposition (USP).
- Products also include a range of factors such as packaging, quality, warranties, after-sales service and branding.

**Pharmaceutical product:** any product, compound, medicine or therapeutic that is subject to regulation as a drug, medicine or controlled substance, used to enhance personal health.

**Service elements of a pharmaceutical product include:**

- Pharmaceutical service
- Presence of instructions on medical application
- Pharmaceutical care and consultations during purchase
- Qualification of pharmaceutical personnel
- Speed of order fulfilment
- Consideration of clients' complaints

### 3. Place

*How does the customer act or buy?*

- Location of purchase
- Ease of transaction
- Access to distribution channels
- Sales force
- Competitor approaches

**Chain:** Company → Distributor → Retailer → End customer

### 4. Promotion

**Ingredients of the promotion mix:**
