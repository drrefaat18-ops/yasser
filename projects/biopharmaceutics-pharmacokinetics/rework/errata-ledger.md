# Errata Ledger

This edition corrects statements that were wrong in the first edition of these notes. Each row below
names the statement, the chapter that now carries the correction, and the source of the corrected
figure. Nothing here reflects on the teachers who wrote the original: several of the changes follow
guidance that was revised after the first edition was printed, and the rest are the kind of slip that
survives any number of readings until someone recalculates it.

A row is `open` until the correction is written and verified in the named chapter, then `fixed`. A row
whose content was removed rather than corrected closes as `removed-with-content`.

Line numbers refer to `ingest/normalized.md`; page numbers to the first-edition PDF.
