# Design — Artificial Intelligence in Health Care: An Interprofessional Introduction

This is the design of the edition built in the first run (approved in chat on 2026-09-24), restated as the harness's
design artifact. The full historical design spec is `history/2026-09-24-interprofessional-rework-design.md`.

## Core shift

The original was "a physician learning AI". This edition is "any health-profession student learning AI": AI literacy
is the content and health care is the context. Every chapter must be readable by a year 1–2 student of medicine,
pharmacy, physical therapy or the health sciences without prior clinical training.

## Structure of every chapter

Opening Case, Learning Objectives (3–5), core sections, the four required boxes (Medical Background in 60 Seconds,
Through Four Lenses, Myth vs Evidence, Safety Alert), Key Takeaways, Self-Assessment (10 MCQs A–D plus one case
question), Answers and Rationales, References. Optional boxes: Deeper Dive, Local Context (Egypt).
