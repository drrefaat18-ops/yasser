# Errata Ledger — Entrepreneurship in Healthcare

Every row is a statement in the source revision notes that this book
changes. `Status` is `open` until the correction is written and verified in the named chapter, then `fixed`; a row
whose content is removed rather than corrected closes as `removed-with-content`.

Line numbers are `ingest/normalized.md`; L1–L3 are the lectures.

| E-001 | F-001 | ch01 | L1, lines 5–10 | Entrepreneurs produce and sell products, bear overhead costs and aim to generate profit. | Entrepreneurship is the discovery, evaluation and exploitation of opportunities to create future goods and services (the opportunity-based view of Shane and Venkataraman, 2000); profit is one aim, and innovation is central. | fixed |
| E-002 | F-002 | ch01 | L1, line 10 | An entrepreneur processes raw materials into finished products. | An entrepreneur may create a product or a service; most health ventures, such as a laboratory or an imaging centre, sell services. | fixed |
